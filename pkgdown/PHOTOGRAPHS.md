# Historical photographs

These photographs provide historical context for Quebec electoral life.
They predate the surveys analyzed by qesR and are not pictures of the survey
respondents or fieldwork. Captions identify the event and date in English
and French, with plain-text photographer and archive credits. Source links
are retained below rather than displayed in the website captions. Images are served locally from
`pkgdown/assets/history/`, which pkgdown copies to the site’s `history/`.
This website-only directory is excluded from the R package build.

- **English and French home pages:** `saint-henri-ballot-count-1938.jpg`.
  Counting ballots in the federal by-election in Saint-Henri, Montréal,
  17 January 1938. Conrad Poirier / Bibliothèque et Archives nationales du
  Québec, P48,S1,P2656. [Source and public-domain record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Le_d%C3%A9pouillement_des_votes.jpg).
  Downloaded the 1280px JPEG derivative of the Commons file (whose contrast
  was adjusted by Kbh3rd in 2009). No further image processing is applied.
- **English and French campaign-transition examples:**
  `saint-laurent-campaign-1944.jpg`. A bus queue in Saint-Laurent with
  Bloc populaire campaign posters on utility poles, 2 August 1944.
  Conrad Poirier / Bibliothèque et Archives nationales du Québec,
  P48,S1,P10504. [Source and public-domain record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Feature._Everybody_Lines_Up_-_Ville_St._Laurent_BAnQ_P48S1P10504.jpg).
  Downloaded the original 960 × 713 JPEG. The people are waiting for a bus,
  not voting; the captions and alt text preserve that distinction.

Both records identify the photographs as public domain in Canada and the
United States. Downloaded 21 November 2026. The website displays each full
photograph without cropping, at a compact size in both light and dark themes.
